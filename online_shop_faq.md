# Online Shop Customer Support FAQ

## Orders & Tracking
- **How do I track my order?**
  Once your order ships, you will receive an email confirmation with a tracking link. You can also view real-time tracking updates under the "My Orders" tab in your customer account.
- **Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?**
  Orders enter our automated fulfillment queue within 30 minutes of placement. You can cancel or change items in your order within 30 minutes via your account dashboard. After 30 minutes, the order cannot be altered, but you may return eligible items upon arrival.

## Shipping & Delivery
- **What shipping options do you offer?**
  We offer Standard Shipping (3-5 business days, $5.99 or FREE on orders over $50) and Express Shipping (1-2 business days, $14.99).
- **Do you ship internationally?**
  Currently, we only ship to the continental United States, Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories. We do not support international shipping at this time.

## Returns & Refunds
- **What is your return policy?**
  We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for standard items in original, unused condition with original packaging. Return shipping is free for store credit; prepaid labels for refunds to original payment methods incur a $4.99 return fee.
- **How long do refunds take to process?**
  Once your return package arrives at our warehouse, inspection takes 2-3 business days. Approved refunds post to your original payment method within 5-7 business days depending on your bank.

## Payment Methods
- **What payment methods do you accept?**
  We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Shop Online Store Gift Cards.
- **Do you accept cryptocurrency or cash on delivery (COD)?**
  No, we do not accept Bitcoin, any cryptocurrency, digital tokens, or Cash on Delivery.

## Account Management & Security
- **How do I reset my account password?**
  Click the "Forgot Password?" link on the login page and enter your registered email address to receive a secure password reset link valid for 24 hours.
- **How can I delete my account?**
  Account deletion requires verification by our privacy and administrative team. Please contact human customer support for assistance with account termination requests.
